# Global Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document Reference: HR-TRV-2025
Effective Date: March 1, 2025

1. Policy Overview and Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and executive leadership traveling on authorized business for the company. All business travel must be pre-approved by a Department Director at least 14 days prior to departure.

2. Air Travel Booking Standards
- Economy Class is mandatory for all domestic flights and international flights under 6 continuous hours in duration.
- Premium Economy is permitted for international flights exceeding 6 hours.
- Business Class is only permitted for flights exceeding 10 continuous flight hours, subject to written approval by the Chief Operating Officer (COO).
- Flight bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal (TripLink) at least 14 days in advance to secure non-peak commercial fares.

3. Lodging and Hotel Guidelines
- Standard single hotel rooms should not exceed $220 per night in North America, €200 per night in Europe, or $180 per night in Asia-Pacific tier-1 cities.
- Luxury hotels (5-star, suites, or boutique resorts) are non-reimbursable without prior Executive Committee authorization.
- Hotel cancellation penalties caused by personal change of mind are the sole responsibility of the employee.

4. Daily Meal and Per Diem Allowances
- The daily meal per diem cap is $85 per day ($20 for breakfast, $25 for lunch, $40 for dinner).
- Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless consumed during an authorized client entertainment dinner with prior manager sign-off.
- Individual itemized receipts are required for any single meal expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips without itemization will be rejected.

5. Expense Submission Deadlines and Auditing
- All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted via the Concur portal within 30 calendar days from the last date of travel.
- Failure to submit claims within 60 days results in permanent forfeiture of reimbursement rights.
- The Finance Audit Team conducts monthly random audits on 15% of all submitted expense reports.
