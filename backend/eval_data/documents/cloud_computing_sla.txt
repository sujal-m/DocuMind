# Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Document Reference: SLA-2025-V3
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Service Commitment & Availability
CloudCore Systems warrants a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95% during any monthly billing cycle. Service availability is measured 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, excluding scheduled maintenance windows. 

Scheduled maintenance occurs every second Sunday between 02:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC and requires 72 hours prior notification to the Customer via dashboard alert and email.

2. Service Credits and Penalties
If CloudCore fails to meet the Monthly Uptime Percentage, Customer is eligible for a Service Credit calculated as follows:
- Monthly Uptime between 99.00% and 99.94%: 10% Service Credit applied to next month's invoice.
- Monthly Uptime between 95.00% and 98.99%: 25% Service Credit applied to next month's invoice.
- Monthly Uptime below 95.00%: 50% Service Credit applied to next month's invoice.

Service credits are non-refundable and can only be applied against future billing charges. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the billing cycle in which the incident occurred.

3. Incident Severity and Response Times
CloudCore commits to the following initial response times based on incident severity:
- Severity 1 (Critical Outage): Core infrastructure down, revenue-impacting. Response time: Within 15 minutes. 24x7 coverage.
- Severity 2 (Major Degradation): High latency, partial service failure with workaround available. Response time: Within 1 hour.
- Severity 3 (Minor Defect): Non-critical feature impairment. Response time: Within 4 business hours.
- Severity 4 (General Inquiry): Configuration questions and enhancement requests. Response time: Within 1 business day.

4. Exclusions and Force Majeure
The Service Level Agreement does not apply to performance issues or downtime resulting from:
a) Customer configurations, custom code, or unsupported third-party software.
b) Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks exceeding 500 Gbps where Customer has not purchased Enterprise Shield protection.
c) DNS failures outside CloudCore's authoritative nameservers.
d) Force majeure events including civil unrest, natural disasters, and global telecommunication backbone severed cables.
