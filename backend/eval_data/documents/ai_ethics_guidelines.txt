# Responsible AI Ethics and Governance Charter
Document Reference: AI-GOV-2025-01
Effective Date: February 15, 2025

1. Purpose and Guiding Principles
The purpose of this Charter is to establish operational safeguards for developing and deploying artificial intelligence models across enterprise operations. All AI systems must adhere to four cardinal pillars: Fairness, Transparency, Accountability, and Robustness.

2. Fairness and Bias Mitigation
All datasets used for training or fine-tuning machine learning models must undergo statistical demographic parity tests before deployment.
- Disparate impact ratio across protected groups must remain between 0.80 and 1.25.
- Annual third-party bias audits are mandatory for any algorithm influencing hiring, credit decisions, or customer risk scoring.
- When bias exceeds acceptable thresholds, the model must be placed in a canary containment mode or rolled back to an approved baseline.

3. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Oversight
Autonomous AI decision-making without direct human oversight is strictly prohibited in Tier-1 high-stakes workflows:
- Medical triage and health recommendations require verification by a licensed medical practitioner.
- Legal contract generation and financial loan rejections above $50,000 require human underwriter sign-off within 24 hours.
- Automated moderation flags with user account suspension consequences must support human appeal within 48 hours.

4. Data Privacy, Lineage, and Compliance
- AI pipelines must comply fully with GDPR, CCPA, and ISO/IEC 42001 AI management standards.
- Personal Identifiable Information (PII) must be masked using differential privacy techniques before ingestion into embedding stores or vector databases.
- Models must not train on customer proprietary data unless an explicit opt-in data licensing consent agreement is executed.
- Complete data lineage logs must be retained for at least 7 years for auditing purposes.

5. Explainability and Incident Reporting
- High-stakes models must output feature attribution scores (SHAP or Integrated Gradients) explaining decision factors.
- Severe AI hallucinations causing financial harm or regulatory breach must be reported to the AI Ethics Board within 12 hours of discovery.
